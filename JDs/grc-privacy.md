# GRC / Privacy Job Descriptions

Representative GRC, Risk, Compliance, and Privacy JDs across entry to leadership levels.

> Companion roadmap: [GRC & Privacy Career Roadmap](../grc-privacy.md)

---

## 1. GRC Analyst (Entry) \u2014 SaaS company

**About the role**

We're hiring a GRC Analyst to support our SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 program. You will be the bridge between security, engineering, legal, and our auditors.

**Responsibilities**

- Collect, organize, and maintain audit evidence in Vanta / Drata / Hyperproof
- Track open audit findings and risk register items to closure
- Respond to customer security questionnaires (SIG, CAIQ, custom)
- Maintain policies, procedures, and the internal control matrix
- Support quarterly access reviews and vendor reviews
- Assist with privacy operations \u2014 DSAR intake, RoPA upkeep

**Required**

- 1\u20132 years in IT, audit, or security operations
- Basic understanding of ISO 27001 or SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria
- Excellent written communication
- Strong spreadsheet skills
- Familiarity with one ticketing system (Jira, ServiceNow)

**Nice to have**

- Pursuing ISO 27001 LA / LI, CompTIA Security+, or CC by ISC2
- Exposure to a GRC platform (Vanta, Drata, Hyperproof, Scrut)

## 2. IT Risk / Compliance Analyst (Mid) \u2014 Fintech

**The role**

You will help the bank's Risk team operationalize cyber and tech risk \u2014 from new product reviews to vendor due diligence to internal audit support.

**Responsibilities**

- Conduct IT and cyber risk assessments using NIST 800-30 / FAIR-lite methodology
- Run Third Party Risk Management (TPRM) reviews \u2014 questionnaires, evidence review, contractual controls
- Maintain the enterprise risk register and KRIs
- Support audits (Internal Audit, RBI / regulator, SOC 2, PCI-DSS)
- Translate technical findings into business language for senior management
- Lead awareness campaigns and policy refresh cycles

**Requirements**

- 3\u20135 years in IT audit, risk, or GRC
- Strong understanding of NIST CSF, ISO 27001, PCI-DSS
- One of CISA, CRISC, ISO 27001 LA, or in progress
- Strong written and verbal communication
- Comfort with a GRC platform (Archer, MetricStream, ServiceNow IRM, OneTrust)

**Bonus**

- BFSI domain knowledge (RBI Master Direction on IT Framework / Cyber Security Framework)
- Privacy certification (CIPM, CIPP/E)

## 3. Senior GRC Manager \u2014 Multi-product SaaS

**Position summary**

You will lead the GRC function for a 500-person company with customers in 40+ countries. You'll own the multi-framework compliance program and partner with sales, customer success, and engineering.

**Key responsibilities**

- Own SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, ISO 27701, PCI-DSS, HIPAA, and emerging frameworks (ISO 42001)
- Drive cross-functional audits with limited disruption to engineering
- Lead enterprise risk reviews; report KRIs to the Audit Committee quarterly
- Manage the security questionnaire response process (10\u201320 per week)
- Oversee TPRM \u2014 vendor onboarding, periodic reviews, exit
- Manage cyber insurance renewal and broker relationships
- Hire and mentor a team of 2\u20134 GRC analysts

**Required**

- 6\u20139 years in GRC / compliance / audit, with 2+ years managing people or programs
- CISA or CISM (one is mandatory)
- Deep familiarity with at least two of: SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI-DSS, HIPAA, FedRAMP
- Comfortable presenting to senior leadership and external auditors
- Excellent written communication

## 4. Privacy Manager / Privacy Counsel \u2014 Global product company

**Description**

The Privacy Manager will run day-to-day privacy operations across our products and support the DPO on regulatory matters. You'll partner with engineering, legal, and security.

**Responsibilities**

- Run DPIAs / PIAs for new features and vendors
- Maintain Records of Processing Activities (RoPA) and data inventory
- Run DSAR / data subject rights workflows
- Support cross-border data transfer programs (SCCs, BCRs, DPF)
- Provide privacy guidance for GenAI and analytics initiatives
- Train product, engineering, and customer-facing teams on privacy

**Required**

- 5\u20138 years in privacy operations, legal, or compliance
- Deep knowledge of GDPR + at least one of CCPA/CPRA, India DPDP Act, LGPD, PIPEDA, PDPA Singapore
- One of CIPP/E, CIPP/US, CIPM, CIPT (multiple preferred)
- Ability to write clear policy and technical guidance for engineering
- Strong stakeholder management

**Bonus**

- Legal degree
- Experience with privacy engineering tooling (OneTrust, Securiti, BigID, DataGrail)
- Experience with privacy-by-design for AI / ML systems

## 5. Head of GRC / Director Risk & Compliance \u2014 BFSI

**Role overview**

The Head of GRC owns the bank's combined cyber, IT, and information risk function. Reports to the CISO. Manages a team of 8\u201315 across audit, risk, compliance, and privacy.

**You will**

- Set the multi-year GRC roadmap aligned to RBI / SEBI / DPDP Act / DORA (for global subs) requirements
- Own the cyber risk appetite framework; quarterly report to the Risk Committee and Board
- Lead regulator interactions and inspections
- Run M&A diligence on the cyber side
- Oversee cyber insurance program
- Hire, mentor, and develop the GRC leadership pipeline

**Required**

- 12+ years total, with 6+ years in leadership roles
- Multiple certifications across CISM, CISA, CRISC, CISSP, ISO 27001 LA
- Demonstrated regulator-facing track record
- Strong commercial + risk acumen
- Board / executive presentation experience

## 6. Data Protection Officer (DPO) \u2014 Multinational product company

**Description**

Acting as the formal DPO under GDPR / DPDP / equivalent, you will independently advise the business on personal data matters and be the registered point of contact with supervisory authorities.

**Responsibilities**

- Independently monitor compliance with GDPR, DPDP, and other privacy laws
- Be the point of contact for supervisory authorities (DPAs) and data subjects
- Review and challenge DPIAs / PIAs
- Oversee data breach notification processes
- Run the privacy training program
- Advise on lawful basis, retention, profiling, automated decision making, AI use cases
- Maintain independence from product / commercial decision-making

**Required**

- 8\u201312+ years in privacy / data protection
- CIPP/E + CIPM strongly preferred; legal background or LL.M (privacy) common
- Detailed knowledge of GDPR, e-Privacy, DPDP, regional equivalents
- Ability to operate with independence from line management
- Strong executive presence

## What recruiters search for (keyword cheatsheet)

- **Frameworks**: ISO 27001/27002/27017/27018/27701/42001, SOC 2 (TSC), NIST CSF 2.0, NIST 800-53/800-171, PCI-DSS 4.0, HIPAA / HITRUST, FedRAMP, CIS Controls v8, COBIT 2019, ISO 22301 (BCM)
- **Privacy laws**: GDPR, UK GDPR + DPA 2018, CCPA / CPRA, India DPDP Act 2023, LGPD, PIPEDA, PDPA Singapore, PIPL (China)
- **Risk methodologies**: NIST 800-30, FAIR (Factor Analysis of Information Risk), OCTAVE, ISO 31000, ISO/IEC 27005
- **GRC platforms**: Vanta, Drata, Secureframe, Sprinto, Scrut, Hyperproof, AuditBoard, Archer, ServiceNow IRM, MetricStream, LogicGate, Thoropass
- **Privacy platforms**: OneTrust, TrustArc, Securiti.ai, BigID, DataGrail, Osano
- **TPRM**: SIG, CAIQ, OneTrust Vendorpedia, ProcessUnity, Prevalent, Whistic, SecurityScorecard, Bitsight
- **Certifications**: CISA, CISM, CRISC, CGEIT, CISSP, CIPP/E, CIPP/US, CIPM, CIPT, ISO 27001 LA/LI, HCISPP, PCI ISA, CDPSE
- **AI governance (emerging)**: ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act, OECD AI Principles
